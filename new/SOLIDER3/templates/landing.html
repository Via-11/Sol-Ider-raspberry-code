Why Sol-ider?

Turning food waste into
a resource, not a problem.

The Philippines generates over 14 million metric tons of food waste annually. Sol-ider addresses this by using solar energy to dehydrate food scraps, reducing volume and enabling reuse as animal feed or compost.

🌍

Reduce Food Waste

Food waste in landfills produces methane — a greenhouse gas 25× more potent than CO₂. Dehydration reduces waste volume by up to 60%, preventing landfill contribution.

☀️

Solar-Powered

100% solar energy operation means zero electricity cost and no carbon footprint. Every batch processed is a net positive for the environment.

📊

Data-Driven Impact

Real-time sensors track every drying cycle. The dashboard shows exactly how much waste was processed, when, and how effectively — making the impact visible and measurable.

♻️

Closed-Loop System

Dehydrated food waste becomes animal feed or fertilizer. The grinder attachment processes dried material into usable powder, completing the waste-to-resource loop.

The Process

How it works

1

Load Food Waste

Place food scraps (target ~5 kg) onto the drying tray above the load cells.

2

Solar Drying Begins

The system automatically detects the batch. Temperature and humidity sensors start tracking.

3

Real-Time Monitoring

The dashboard shows live temperature, humidity, and weight as drying progresses.

4

Auto-Detection

When humidity drops to ≤30% and weight reaches ≤2 kg, the batch is marked dried and recorded.

5

Grind & Reuse

Dried material is processed by the grinder into powder for use as feed or compost.

Hardware

Components

  • 🖥
    Raspberry Pi 4
    Main controller running Flask web server & SQLite database
  • 🌡
    DHT22 × 2
    Dual sensors for redundant temperature & humidity measurement
  • ⚖
    HX711 + Load Cells × 4
    Four-corner load sensing for accurate weight (up to 6 kg), averaged into one reading
  • 📺
    SH1106 OLED
    On-device display showing live readings and drying status
